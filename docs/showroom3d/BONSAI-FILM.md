# Bonsai Hub film: "One system. All your business." / "نظام واحد. كل أعمالك."

A 60 second promotional film for the big screen of the VIP room (and anywhere else): what the Bonsai Hub, the internal business platform of The Closets / YAS Wood, can do. Files are in `public/yas-bloom/film/` (served as `/yas-bloom/film/`), the build scripts in `scripts/bonsai-film/`.

## Read this first

* **It is a product film, not an AI film.** Every screen in it is the real Bonsai Hub interface (the real React components of the Hub repo, bundled and driven in headless Chromium) filled with **synthetic demo data** (invented project and staff names, invented figures). Nothing is generated by an AI model, no customer, no phone number, no email, no price of a real customer, no credential and no live data is shown. The on-screen note "Illustrative demo data / بيانات توضيحية" says so on every screen scene.
* So `films.json` says `real: true` (it is not an AI illustration and carries no "AI-generated illustration" label) and the player's "Company film" tag applies.
* **No voice-over.** The story is told by on-screen text, English first line, Arabic second line (right-to-left, shaped by the browser, IBM Plex Sans Arabic). The Arabic lines are written by the assistant and **await native review** (same rule as the other VIP films).
* **The music is synthesised locally** (numpy, no samples, no licensed music, no AI service): an original D-minor ambient bed, see "Audio". It is tasteful and understated but it is not a composer's work. It can be replaced with Higgsfield music later (see the end of this file).

## How it was made (reproducible)

1. `scripts/bonsai-film/` (committed): `film.html` (the whole film as one deterministic HTML page: `renderAt(t)` draws second t; 1920x1080), `render.cjs` (Playwright, frame by frame, 24 fps), `synth_music.py`, `encode.sh`, `make_vtt.py`, `cues.json`.
2. Raw material (NOT committed, about 400 MB): image sequences recorded from the Hub harnesses with a real mouse in Chromium at 2x device scale, dark theme: the Lead Roadmap drag (144 frames), the Studio price / publish / audit session (300 frames), stills in Arabic and on a phone viewport. The harness entries were copied with their sample names replaced by invented ones (Palm Residence, Cedar Villa, ... staff Nora Lane, Omar Vale, Lina Brook, Zayn Hart) and Arabic faces loaded from `@fontsource/ibm-plex-sans-arabic`. The Hub repo itself was not modified.
3. `node render.cjs <work> 0 1440` (4 parallel workers), `bash encode.sh <work> <out>`, `python3 make_vtt.py <out>`.
4. Look: dark walnut / bronze / amber with the Bonsai orange, Fraunces headlines, slow 3D-tilted camera moves, edge blur (depth of field), light sweeps at each scene change, floating dust, 24 fps subtle grain (ffmpeg `noise`), fade from / to black.

## Audio

Synthesised by `synth_music.py`: additive pad on a Dm9 - Bbmaj7 - Gm9 - A progression (5 s per chord) that resolves to D major on the end card, sub drone, a soft heartbeat pulse that fades in at 6 s, sparse FM bells (sparser early and late), a rising swell into the title hit at 3.5 s, quiet whooshes at the scene changes, three soft UI ticks (card drop, price save, publish), a synthetic hall reverb, 0.4 s fade in, 1.4 s fade out. Loudness measured on the final MP4 with ebur128: **-16.0 LUFS integrated, loudness range 3.6 LU, sample peak -5.5 dBFS** (no clipping). I could not listen to it; the numbers, the spectrogram and the structure were checked, not the taste. If it sounds thin or dull on the big screen, regenerate it (the script is deterministic) or replace it.

## What a professional voice-over / music needs (Higgsfield credits, rough)

The account has 2.06 credits; none were spent here. For reference, this project's own log (`film-src/generations.json`, Journey film) paid 8.19 credits for 128 s of ElevenLabs Music v2 and 3.15 credits for 112 s of Text to Speech V2 narration. Scaled to this film: a 60 s music bed is about **4 credits** (plus 1 to 2 for a second take), an Arabic voice-over of about 130 words (60 s) about **1.5 to 2 credits**, an English one the same, so **roughly 10 to 12 credits** for music + both voice-overs with two takes each. Quote with the tool first, prices move. A new music or voice file only needs `encode.sh` to be re-run with a different `music.wav` (mix the voice under the same -16 LUFS target).

## Wiring in the VIP room

* `films.json` has the entry `bonsai-hub` (`real: true`, `order: 3`), so `parseFilmsManifest` sorts it after the two real company films and before the AI documentary (`journey`, `real: false`, `order: 99`). The player (`filmPlayer.js`) loads it into its library like the others.
* **The agenda is not data-driven**, so `films.json` alone does not make the film reachable from the meeting (the film is in the player's library, but no slide points at it). `vip/meeting.js` hard-codes `REAL_FILMS` (two entries), the meeting deck's film slides (`...REAL_FILMS.map(...)`), `AGENDA` (six items with fixed slide numbers) and `agendaIndex()`. Another branch (`claude/web-vip-film-preload`) owns the film player code at the moment, and the brief for this change forbids touching it, so `meeting.js` was **not changed**.
