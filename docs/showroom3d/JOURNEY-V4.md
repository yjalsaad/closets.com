# Film v4: v3 with the leaders' scenes animated with AI from the real photographs

v4 = v3 with the four leaders' shots (22.8 s) replaced by **AI-animated clips of the two real photographs** (Kling 3.0 image-to-video, silent, standard mode), placed in the same AI-generated empty office / boardroom with the same layered parallax. Everything else is unchanged: same 58 shots and timings, **same narration and music (decoded audio identical to v2 and v3), same 219.0 s, same caption timing** (`journey4.en.vtt` and `journey4.ar.vtt` equal v3's). v2 and v3 are untouched fallbacks.

## How the animated shots are built

* Clips (Kling 3.0 std, 5 s, silent, from the photographs as start frames): `leaderA_1` and `leaderB_1` (generated by the coordinator; clip A starts on a blink with the head tilted and then turns away from the camera), and two more made by this build with calmer prompts, `leaderA_2` and `leaderB_2` (both start in the photograph's own pose and end looking at the camera). **`leaderA_1` is not used in the film**: mid-turn its likeness was the weakest (head turned away, half-closed eyes, a different look from the photograph).
* Shot plan (`PLAN` in `scripts/showroom3d-film/build_film4.py`), one clip per leader per shot, slowed with frame blending (no stepping) and the last frame held while the push-in continues: G01 (134.0 s, both, 6.7 s) A2 at 0.75 speed and B1 from 0.35 s at 0.72; I01 (170.4 s, leader A alone, 5.8 s) A2 from 0.3 s at 0.82; I05 (183.5 s, leader B alone, 8.7 s) B2 at 0.62 (8.1 s of motion, then the held last frame); J03 (204.1 s, both, 4.9 s) A2 from 1.6 s at 0.72 and B2 from 1.5 s at 0.78. A first version dissolved between two clips of the same person inside a shot; frames showed two head poses ghosting into a smeared face, so it was removed. Nothing is looped and no clip is cut at a visible jump.
* The framed clip drifts one way, the AI-generated room the other (as v3), with a 6% slow push inside the frame (decoded 8% larger, so nothing is upscaled). The same "mod" colour grade as v3.
* The same clip appears more than once across the four shots (there are only four clips); the repeats use different parts of the clip, speeds and framing.

## Verification

* `ffprobe` both files: H.264 High, yuv420p, 24 fps, AAC-LC stereo 48 kHz, **219.0 s**, `moov` before `mdat` (faststart); full `ffmpeg` decode of both: 0 errors.
* Loudness (`ebur128` on the decoded AAC): 1080p **-16.1 LUFS**, 720p -16.1; true peak -2.2 / -1.9 dBFS; LRA 1.7 LU. Decoded audio equals v3's (and v2's): -104 dB difference. `journey4.en.vtt` and `journey4.ar.vtt` are byte-identical to v3's (51 and 47 cues, English at most 2 lines of 42 characters).
* Frames: 24-frame contact sheet of the finished 1080p file, and 8 frames of the leaders' shots at 1080p (`journey4-leaders-frames.jpg`): no distortion of the clips (they are drawn at their own proportions), the badge is clear of the frames, the end card shows the new label, the disclaimer and the credits.
* Jest: `journey4.test.js` (label wording, 219 s, sizes, captions equal to v3, 42-character limit), `journey3.test.js`, `preloadAssets`, `vipMeeting`, `vipFilm` pass (see the PR description for the counts). Five suites that need `gsap` cannot load in this checkout (shared `node_modules`), unrelated.
* Not run: `vipprofile.js media` (reads `films.json` only), HTTP range requests.
* Disk: the shared disk filled up twice during the build; the picture master was re-rendered at a higher intermediate CRF (`FILM_CRF=18`, an env variable added to `build_film2.py`) before the final two-pass encode, which sets the delivered quality (same bitrates as v2 and v3).
